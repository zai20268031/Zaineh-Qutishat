Simulation & Digital Twin Methodology

1. Dwell-Time Multiplier: Lower traffic speeds increase driver exposure time. Calculated as 1 + ((100 - Speed) / 100) × 0.8 (ranging from 1.0x at 100 km/h up to 1.76x in gridlock).

2. Contextual Programmatic Triggers: Advertisers submit base CPM bids plus contextual boosts:

  • Heatwave (>30°C): Cold Beverage & HVAC brands apply a +65% bid multiplier.
  • Cold Snap (<5°C): Hot Coffee & Winter Apparel apply a +55% bid multiplier.
  • Smog Alert (AQI >150): Health & Air Purifier brands boost bids by +70%.
  • Gridlock (<25 km/h): Food Delivery & Streaming apps boost bids by +40%.

3. Second-Price Auction & Floor Governance: Eligible bids above the Minimum CPM Bid Floor compete. The winner pays the second-highest bid + $0.10 (or the floor). If no advertiser meets the floor, the billboard displays an unpaid House / PSA fallback ad, lowering the Fill Rate.
